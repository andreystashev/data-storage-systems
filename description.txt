Data Storage Systems: course assignment

Design a system of your choice and describe its architecture.

Deliverables:
- Describe each service, its responsibility, and its container image name.
- Draw a high-level design (HLD) diagram.
- Include at least three services, external dependencies (REST APIs or data
  sources), a database, and a message queue.
- Describe services running outside Kubernetes: their ports, host placement,
  and system requirements.
- Use at least Deployment, Ingress, Service, ConfigMap, Secret, and Job or
  CronJob resources.
- Specify container resource requests and limits.

This file is an English translation of the original assignment. It describes
course requirements, not a claim that every requirement is implemented in the
checked-in manifests.
